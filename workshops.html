Workshops

Interactive financial literacy sessions, tailored to the needs of our community — and always 100% free.

We host regular workshops covering a range of financial topics, specifically tailored to meet the needs of the Punjabi immigrant community. These sessions are interactive, ensuring participants gain practical knowledge they can apply immediately. Workshops are delivered in Punjabi and English, and attendees are encouraged to bring their real-life financial questions.

What We Cover

Topics & Curriculum

Curriculum is approved by a Community Development Banking VP and Certified Financial Coach

Budgeting & Saving

Build a household budget, set up an emergency fund, and stretch every paycheck further.

Investing Basics

Understand stocks, index funds, and how to grow long-term wealth in the U.S. market.

Credit & Debt

Build credit from scratch, avoid predatory loans, and manage debt responsibly.

Retirement Planning

Navigate 401(k)s, IRAs, and Social Security — and plan for a secure future.

Banking 101

Open accounts, understand fees, send money home safely, and use online banking with confidence.

Taxes & Filing

Demystify the IRS, file returns correctly, and claim every credit you're entitled to.

What To Expect At A Workshop

Our workshops are practical, friendly, and judgement-free. Whether you're brand new to America or have been here for years, you'll walk away with tools you can use the same day.

Every workshop includes

  • Sessions in Punjabi and English
  • Easy-to-follow handouts you keep
  • Real-world examples from our community
  • Q&A with our volunteers
  • Optional one-on-one follow-up
  • Snacks, chai, and a welcoming environment
