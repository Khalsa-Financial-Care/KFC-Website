Our Mission

Empowering new Punjabi immigrants with the financial knowledge and tools necessary to thrive in America.

At Khalsa Financial Care, we believe that financial literacy is one of the most powerful tools an immigrant can carry into a new country. Yet for many new Punjabi families arriving in America, the U.S. financial system can feel intimidating, confusing, and inaccessible.

We exist to change that. Through free workshops, easy-to-understand resources, and one-on-one guidance, we help immigrants navigate everything from opening their first bank account to planning for retirement — so they can build generational stability and pursue the American dream on their own terms.

We envision a community where every new immigrant is equipped with the knowledge to make informed financial decisions. By providing education on essential topics such as budgeting, saving, investing, and retirement planning, we aim to bridge the knowledge gap and foster financial independence.
