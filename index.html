Our Mission

Bridging the Knowledge Gap

At Khalsa Financial Care, we envision a community where every new immigrant is equipped with the knowledge to make informed financial decisions. By providing education on essential topics — budgeting, saving, investing, and retirement planning — we bridge the knowledge gap and foster financial independence across generations.

No one should have to navigate a new financial system alone.

Workshops

Interactive sessions covering budgeting, saving, investing, and retirement planning — tailored to our community.

Resources

Easy-to-understand guides and materials, available in plain English and Punjabi, to support continued learning.

One-on-One Guidance

Personalized support to help individuals and families reach their unique financial goals.

Our Approach

Built For Our Community. Rooted In Trust.

We meet families where they are — culturally, linguistically, and financially. Our volunteers speak Punjabi and English, and every session is designed to feel like a conversation, not a classroom.

  • Free for everyone — no fees, no upsells, no catch
  • Punjabi & English — learn comfortably in the language you prefer
  • Practical, not theoretical — real tools you can apply the same day
  • Culturally aware — we understand sending money home, joint families, and more
  • Always confidential — your finances stay between you and us
What We Teach

Financial Topics We Cover

From your first bank account to your retirement plan — we've got every step covered.

Budgeting & Saving

Build a household budget, set up an emergency fund, and stretch every paycheck further.

Investing Basics

Understand stocks, index funds, and how to grow long-term wealth in the U.S. market.

Credit & Debt

Build credit from scratch, avoid predatory loans, and manage debt responsibly.

Retirement Planning

Navigate 401(k)s, IRAs, and Social Security — and plan for a secure future.

Banking 101

Open accounts, understand fees, send money home safely, and use online banking with confidence.

Taxes & Filing

Demystify the IRS, file returns correctly, and claim every credit you're entitled to.
